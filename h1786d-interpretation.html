VWF H1786D: evidence interpretation

Structural effect suggested · Clinical pathogenicity not established

What does this substitution do?

Histidine (H) at VWF position 1786 is replaced by aspartate (D), changing local chemistry from an imidazole-containing side chain to a negatively charged carboxylate at physiological pH. Position 1786 lies in the collagen-interacting A3 region. This substitution is therefore a plausible perturbation of collagen recognition, but the substitution alone cannot establish a functional or clinical effect.

Integrated interpretation

Mechanistic hypothesis: H1786D may alter local VWF A3–collagen interactions. Evidence strength: the structural location and residue mapping are established, but a quantified energetic consequence, evolutionary conservation and clinical phenotype are not established by this page. Conclusion: prioritize collagen-binding disruption as a testable hypothesis, not a confirmed diagnosis.

Clinical classification requires independently traceable functional and patient evidence. This interpretation deliberately excludes molecular dynamics simulations.
